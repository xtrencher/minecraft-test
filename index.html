Voxelands

      
A tiny procedural voxel world, built for the browser.

      

        WASD move   Mouse look   Space jump / swim

        Shift sneak   Ctrl or double-tap W sprint

        Left Click mine / attack (hold)   Right Click place / use / eat

        1-9 / Scroll hotbar   E inventory & crafting   Q drop item

        Grenade: hold Right Click to charge, release to throw   Pistol / Bazooka: Right Click fires

        Machine Gun: hold Right Click for auto fire   Sniper: Right Click scopes, Left Click fires

        Airstrike: aim the laser, Right Click to call it in

        Creative: double-tap Space to fly, middle click picks a block

        F1 hide HUD   F3 debug info   F5 camera view   Esc pause menu & settings

WebGL 2 Required

      

        Voxelands needs a browser with WebGL 2 support (any recent version of
        Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Please update your browser, or enable
        hardware acceleration in its settings, and reload the page.
      

    

  


  

    

      
Keyboard & Mouse Required

      

        Voxelands is a first-person 3D game that needs a physical keyboard and mouse
        for movement and looking around. Please open this page on a desktop or laptop
        computer to play.

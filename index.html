WebGL 2 Required

Voxelands needs a browser with WebGL 2 support (any recent version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari). Please update your browser, or enable hardware acceleration in its settings, and reload the page.

Keyboard & Mouse Required

Voxelands is a first-person 3D game that needs a physical keyboard and mouse for movement and looking around. Please open this page on a desktop or laptop computer to play.
